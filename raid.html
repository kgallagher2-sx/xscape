Gloamhold

A sealed crypt-fortress under a drowned moon. Clear its rooms and defeat Morvane the Unsleeping to open the Vault Chest. Starter kit makes a clean clear unlikely — Black d'hide from the Shop, plus vault-forged kit and clean execution, can just clear it.

Rooms, in order: The Veil Gate → Supply Alcove → The Wailing Court → Supply Alcove → The Eclipse Corridor → The Last Alcove → The Hollow Throne → The Vault Chest. A small map at the top shows where you are. Dying lets you retry the room from when you entered it, or return to the hub. Loot still needs a Morvane kill.

Move: tap a floor tile to walk (one tile per tick, two if Run is on). A yellow X marks your destination. Keyboard A/D or arrow keys also work. Watch telegraphs and walk off marked tiles before they land.

Prayers — switch before the hit. Keys 1 / 2 / 3, or the buttons.

Magic (1): a violet rear-up, arms lifting, airy whoosh. Protect from Magic.

Missiles (2): a shard throw and a sharper mid cue. Protect from Missiles.

Melee (3): a sword slam and a low thud. Protect from Melee. Cinder Shades always melee.

The Veil Gate: rune tiles light in a sequence — step them in order (three rounds). Gloom lances mark your tile and land two ticks later; walk off. Then step the exit tile on the right.

The Wailing Court: the Pale Warden swaps Magic / Missiles with two-tick tells. Shades must be killed; if they live too long they heal him or explode on you.

The Eclipse Corridor: waves of marked tiles. Dodge, then step the exit.

Morvane — four phases (by remaining HP):

Twin Crowns (above 55%): Magic / Missiles, slower three-tick tells.

Court of Shades (28–55%): melee joins, faster tells. Shades spawn. Stand on the crown sigil or take Judgment. Don't stand still.

Nightfall (10–28%): quicker flicks, sometimes a one-tick tell, and an occasional Magic → Missiles → Melee combo. After a hit, your tile is marked — walk off. Seek the moon tile or your prayer drains. Rows of tiles go unsafe.

The Eye Opens (last 10%): enrage. Very fast, one-tick tells. Eye beams mark your tile. Prayer drains faster. Stillness is punished sooner.

Supplies: chests along the way are limited. F / Shark heals 20. P / Prayer pot restores 31 prayer. B / Brew heals 16 but costs 5 prayer. R / Range pot boosts your ranged. On-screen buttons work too. Each bite or sip delays your next shot.

Loot: the vault always drops a Gloamhold unique (a small chance of a second) plus GP. Mid pieces can appear in earlier chests. Spend GP in the Shop on Black d'hide. New gear goes to your bank — open Equipment on the hub to put it on.
