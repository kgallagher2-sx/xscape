# Xscape future ideas

Brainstorm list for aligning content in future releases. Nothing here is approved until Kellen marks it.
Started 12 Nov 2026.

**Status key:** ⭐ Top pick · ✅ Kellen likes it · 💡 Proposed

## Guiding principles (Kellen, 12 Nov 2026)
- **Goal:** make the game sticky. Give players a reason to "hop on for a minute" that turns into hours, and reasons to pull friends in.
- **Be our own game.** Borrow the feel of OSRS where it's worth it, but avoid copying more of its systems. Prefer ideas that grow from Xscape's core: short, hard boss fights on a phone.
- **Leveling:** any new skill levels more like World of Warcraft than RuneScape. A small grind is fine and keeps people coming back (Kellen, 12 Nov): early levels come quickly, the last few take a real but reasonable push, and there's no 99-style wall.

## Endless / time-sink modes

### ⭐ The Deep
An endless descending dungeon built from our bosses.
- Each floor takes 1-2 minutes.
- Between floors, pick 1 of 3 random boons.
- Death keeps only part of the loot unless you cash out at a camp floor.
- Duo dives, and a weekly depth leaderboard per friend group.
- Drops infusion essences and expedition maps. Rift modifiers become floor types.
- ✅ **Mining (Kellen):** rare ores found in the Deep. See Skills.

### ✅ Relics (found in the Deep)
Set pieces that drop only at certain depths (e.g. floor 40+), which gives runs a long-term goal.
- Relics are slot items, with up to 3 equipped. They change how you play rather than just adding stats, and they work everywhere.
- Examples:
  - **Ember Heart:** your first hit sets the target burning.
  - **Hollow Crown:** survive at 1 HP once per fight.
  - **Gale Feather:** 2-tile movement for 3 s after dodging a special.
- Duplicates from deeper floors upgrade a relic.

### 💡 Rifts
A daily remixed boss run with random modifiers (e.g. burning tiles, the boss heals when you pray).
- One shared daily seed, so friends compare times.

### 💡 Gauntlet of champions
A ladder of AI duelists, each with a personality and a fixed build.
- Beating one unlocks their gimmick as an infusion.
- Ranks reset weekly.

## Minigames

### ✅ Siege
Up to 4 friends defend the hub gate against escalating waves, with a boss every 5 waves.
- **Kellen:** add strategy and a points system. Points go toward reinforcing defenses, unlocking catapults and hiring archers for the wall.
- Rewards could be cosmetic armor or something useful.
- ✅ **Wall stones:** also earned in Siege, and spent building a personal or clan fort at the hub (bank chest, brew station, trophy room).

### 💡 Caravans
Co-op escort runs between towns.
- Choose the route's risk. Ambushes scale with the cargo's value.
- Friends ride along as guards.
- Sell the cargo at the end for a big payout.

### 💡 Fishing duels
A calm reaction minigame for short bursts.
- Rare catches feed brews and food.

## Skills & gathering

### ✅ Hunts
Track a rare creature across a procedural wilds map using clues (tracks, branches, blood trails), then fight it.
- Each hunt takes 3-5 minutes.
- Shiny variants, and a hunting log to complete.
- **Kellen:** collect hides to craft gear or sell to a trader.

### 💡 Farming and Herblore (OSRS-like, lower priority)
- Herb and seed patches at the hub that grow in real time (20-80 min). Log in to harvest and replant.
- Brew potions from farmed herbs instead of only buying them. This ties skilling to bossing.

### ✅ Mining (Kellen, 12 Nov)
Introduced through the Deep.
- Some floors have ore veins to mine between fights. Mining takes a few ticks and can wake nearby enemies (risk vs. reward).
- Rarer ores sit deeper (working names): iron on floors 1-10, emberite on 20+, voidstone on 40+.
- Ore feeds smithing gear, relic upgrades and infusion ingredients.
- Levels WoW-style, with a small grind at the top end.

### 💡 Group gathering
Shared skilling spots that yield faster with more players nearby.

## Crafting & gear progression

### ✅ Infusion forge
Salvage boss drops into essences that add traits to gear (e.g. +10% vs flyers, heal on kill).
- **Kellen:** infusions are timed and run out, so players keep breaking down armor to stock supplies for the next one.

## Idle / check-in systems

### ✅ Expeditions
Send a hired companion on a 1-8 hour trip for materials. Lend companions to friends for better returns.
- **Kellen:** about once an hour the companion sends a distress call (e.g. "We're out looking for rare herbs and 3 ghouls cornered us on the trail, please send help!").
- Answering a call means a short fight. The more you help, the better the final haul.

## Events

### 💡 Living world events
Random 10-minute hub events that reward being online with friends.
- Examples: a meteor lands, a wandering merchant shows up, a boss breaks through.

### 💡 World boss
A weekly server-wide boss with shared HP.
- A server-wide reward when it dies.

---

## Social & competitive

### 💡 Ghost challenges
Save your best kill as a replay ghost and send it to a friend to beat.

### 💡 Bounty contracts
Opt-in contracts to beat a named friend's ghost or score.
- The friend is notified when you claim one.

### 💡 Bonded duo fights
Two-player-only bosses with split roles (e.g. one player holds a lantern that reveals the boss).

### 💡 Daily duo boss
A rotating partner-only boss.
- A bonus chest for the first kill each day.
- An extra reward when paired with a newer player.

### 💡 Arena seasons
Ranked 1v1 and 2v2 with normalized loadouts.
- Monthly seasons with season cosmetics.

### 💡 Clans
A shared clan patch or clan citadel. Group contributions unlock shared perks or cosmetics.

### 💡 Invite rewards
Both players get a cosmetic when the invited friend earns their first fire cape.

## Retention & collection
- ✅ **Streaks and dailies** (Kellen: pair with Expeditions). Daily or weekly bounties are paid in a token for cosmetics or pets. Missing a day doesn't wipe progress.
- ✅ **Collection log** (Kellen: must have).
- ✅ **Boss pets** (Kellen: must have). Rare drops that follow the player around the hub.
- 💡 **Awakened bosses:** 50 kills unlock a harder version with new attacks, its own pet and a cosmetic.
- 💡 **Companion bond:** expedition companions level up, gain traits, and can join you in the Deep as a weak helper.
- 💡 **Trophy room:** the fort shows boss heads, best times and your rarest drop. Friends can visit.
- 💡 **Lore scraps:** pages from the Deep and Hunts reveal the bosses' story. Finishing a chapter unlocks a title.
- 💡 **Weather and night cycle:** some hunts and events only happen at night or in storms.

## Design checklist (what makes it sticky)
Variable rewards, near misses, streaks/dailies, short loops inside long goals, collections, social obligation, visible progress, status, resetting timers, comeback help.
**Never (Kellen, 12 Nov):** no gimmick or predatory monetization (paid loot boxes, pay-to-refill energy, money-driven FOMO).
