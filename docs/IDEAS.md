# Xscape future ideas

Brainstorm list for aligning content in future releases. Nothing here is approved until Kellen marks it.
Started 12 Nov 2026.

## Goal
Make the game sticky: a reason to "hop on for a minute" that turns into hours, and reasons to pull friends in.

## Guiding principle (Kellen, 12 Nov 2026)
Be our own game. Borrow the feel of OSRS where it's worth it, but avoid copying more of its systems. Prefer ideas that grow from Xscape's core: short, hard boss fights on a phone.

**Leveling rule (Kellen, 12 Nov 2026):** any new skill levels like World of Warcraft, not RuneScape. Fast, satisfying levels; the game is not meant to be a grind.

## Original ideas (preferred direction)
- **Rifts**: daily remixed boss run with random modifiers (e.g. burning tiles, boss heals when you pray). One shared daily seed so friends compare times. Status: proposed.
- **Ghost challenges**: save your best kill as a replay ghost and send it to a friend to beat. Status: proposed.
- **Expeditions**: send a hired companion on 1-8 hour trips for materials; lend companions to friends for better returns. Status: Kellen likes it (keep in pocket).
  - Kellen's twist: about once an hour the companion sends a distress call (e.g. "We're out looking for rare herbs and 3 ghouls cornered us on the trail, please send help!"). Answering it (a short fight) raises the final reward; the more you help, the better the haul.
- **Infusion forge**: salvage boss drops into essences that add traits to gear (e.g. +10% vs flyers, heal on kill). Status: Kellen likes it (keep in pocket).
  - Kellen's twist: infusions are timed and run out, so players keep breaking down armor to stock supplies for the next one.
- **World boss**: weekly server-wide boss with shared HP; server-wide reward on death. Status: proposed.
- **Bonded duo fights**: two-player-only bosses with split roles (e.g. one holds a lantern that reveals the boss). Status: proposed.

## Time-sink candidates (the main ask: play for hours)
- **The Deep**: endless descending dungeon built from our bosses. Floors take 1-2 minutes. Pick 1 of 3 random boons between floors. Death keeps only part of the loot unless you cash out at a camp floor. Duo dives, weekly depth leaderboard per friend group. Drops infusion essences and expedition maps; Rift modifiers become floor types. Status: **Kellen's favorite (12 Nov) — top candidate.**
  - Relic hunting: set pieces drop only at certain depths (e.g. floor 40+), giving runs a long-term goal. Status: **Kellen loves it; needs a purpose.**
  - Proposal: relics are slot items (up to 3 equipped) that change play, not just stats, and work everywhere. Examples: Ember Heart (first hit sets target burning), Hollow Crown (survive at 1 HP once per fight), Gale Feather (2-tile movement for 3 s after dodging a special). Duplicates from deeper floors upgrade them.

- **Hunts**: track a rare creature across a procedural wilds map using clues (tracks, branches, blood trails), then fight it. 3-5 min each. Shiny variants and a hunting log to complete. Status: **Kellen likes it.**
  - Kellen: collect hides to craft gear or sell to a trader.
- **Siege**: up to 4 friends defend the hub gate against escalating waves with a boss every 5 waves; spend wave coins on towers, traps, and buffs between waves. Status: **Kellen likes it.**
  - Kellen: add strategy and a points system; spend points to reinforce defenses, unlock catapults, hire archers for the wall. It's a minigame; rewards could be cosmetic armor or something useful.
  - Proposal: also award wall stones to build a personal or clan fort at the hub (bank chest, brew station, trophy room).
- **Gauntlet of champions**: ladder of AI duelists with personalities and fixed builds; beating one unlocks their gimmick as an infusion; ranks reset weekly. Status: proposed.
- **Bounty contracts**: opt-in contracts to beat a named friend's ghost or score; the friend is notified when you claim it. Status: proposed.

- **Caravans**: co-op escort runs between towns; choose route risk, ambushes scale with cargo value; friends ride as guards; sell cargo for a big payout. Status: proposed.
- **Arena seasons**: ranked 1v1/2v2 with normalized loadouts, monthly seasons, season cosmetics. Status: proposed.
- **Fishing duels**: calm reaction minigame for short bursts; rare catches feed brews and food. Status: proposed.
- **Living world events**: random 10-minute hub events (meteor, wandering merchant, boss breaks through) that reward being online with friends. Status: proposed.

## Hook skill candidates (OSRS-like, lower priority)
- **Farming** (working name): herb/seed patches at the hub that grow in real time (20-80 min). Log in to harvest and replant. Status: proposed.
- **Herblore**: brew the potions from farmed herbs instead of only buying them; ties skilling to bossing. Status: proposed.

## Social / play-with-friends
- Shared clan patch or clan citadel: group contributions unlock shared perks or cosmetics. Status: proposed.
- Daily duo boss: rotating partner-only boss, first daily kill bonus chest, extra reward when paired with a newer player. Status: proposed.
- Invite rewards: both players get a cosmetic when the invited friend earns their first fire cape. Status: proposed.
- Group gathering: shared skilling spots that yield faster with more players nearby. Status: proposed.

## Retention hooks
- Daily/weekly bounties paid in a cosmetic/pet token. Status: proposed.
- Boss pets: rare drops that follow the player in the hub. Status: proposed.
