# Xscape future ideas

Brainstorm list for aligning content in future releases. Nothing here is approved until Kellen marks it.
Started 12 Nov 2026.

## Goal
Make the game sticky: a reason to "hop on for a minute" that turns into hours, and reasons to pull friends in.

## Guiding principle (Kellen, 12 Nov 2026)
Be our own game. Borrow the feel of OSRS where it's worth it, but avoid copying more of its systems. Prefer ideas that grow from Xscape's core: short, hard boss fights on a phone.

## Original ideas (preferred direction)
- **Rifts**: daily remixed boss run with random modifiers (e.g. burning tiles, boss heals when you pray). One shared daily seed so friends compare times. Status: proposed.
- **Ghost challenges**: save your best kill as a replay ghost and send it to a friend to beat. Status: proposed.
- **Expeditions**: send a hired companion on 1-8 hour trips for materials; lend companions to friends for better returns. Status: Kellen likes it (keep in pocket).
  - Kellen's twist: about once an hour the companion sends a distress call (e.g. "We're out looking for rare herbs and 3 ghouls cornered us on the trail, please send help!"). Answering it (a short fight) raises the final reward; the more you help, the better the haul.
- **Infusion forge**: salvage boss drops into essences that add traits to gear (e.g. +10% vs flyers, heal on kill). Status: Kellen likes it (keep in pocket).
  - Kellen's twist: infusions are timed and run out, so players keep breaking down armor to stock supplies for the next one.
- **World boss**: weekly server-wide boss with shared HP; server-wide reward on death. Status: proposed.
- **Bonded duo fights**: two-player-only bosses with split roles (e.g. one holds a lantern that reveals the boss). Status: proposed.

## Time-sink candidates (the main ask: play for hours)
- **The Deep**: endless descending dungeon built from our bosses. Floors take 1-2 minutes. Pick 1 of 3 random boons between floors. Death keeps only part of the loot unless you cash out at a camp floor. Duo dives, weekly depth leaderboard per friend group. Drops infusion essences and expedition maps; Rift modifiers become floor types. Status: proposed.

## Hook skill candidates (OSRS-like, lower priority)
- **Farming** (working name): herb/seed patches at the hub that grow in real time (20-80 min). Log in to harvest and replant. Status: proposed.
- **Herblore**: brew the potions from farmed herbs instead of only buying them; ties skilling to bossing. Status: proposed.

## Social / play-with-friends
- Shared clan patch or clan citadel: group contributions unlock shared perks or cosmetics. Status: proposed.
- Daily duo boss: rotating partner-only boss, first daily kill bonus chest, extra reward when paired with a newer player. Status: proposed.
- Invite rewards: both players get a cosmetic when the invited friend earns their first fire cape. Status: proposed.
- Group gathering: shared skilling spots that yield faster with more players nearby. Status: proposed.

## Retention hooks
- Daily/weekly bounties paid in a cosmetic/pet token. Status: proposed.
- Boss pets: rare drops that follow the player in the hub. Status: proposed.
