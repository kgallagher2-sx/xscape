Watch Magmor

Watch Magmor's body — and listen. Switch your protection prayer before he hits.

Magic: he rears up on his hind legs and lifts his arms. You'll hear a whooshy, fiery wind-up. Protect from Magic.

Missiles: he slams down and stomps the ground. You'll hear a heavy low thud. Protect from Missiles.

Melee: he lunges forward with his claws. Protect from Melee.

Your crossbow fires on its own. At half health, healers appear — tap them to pull them off Magmor.

You can tap a floor tile to step left or right (or use A/D on a keyboard). A yellow X marks your destination. Later bosses will throw fireballs onto your square — walk off before they land. Magmor does not use those attacks.

Eat sharks (heal 20) and sip prayer potions if you slip — each one lands on the next tick and delays your next shot, so eat back up above 70 after a hit. Prayer switching is free.

How to play

Xscape is a portrait, tick-based fight game in the style of a classic tick-based bossing game. Each fight has its own i button with a full guide — this page covers the shared controls.

Move: tap a floor tile to walk (one tile per tick, two if Run is on). A yellow X marks your destination. Keyboard A/D or arrow keys also work.

Prayers — switch before the hit. Keys 1 / 2 / 3, or the on-screen buttons. Listen for the tell.

Magic (1) · Missiles (2) · Melee (3)

Supplies: sharks heal 20 (F). Prayer pots restore 31 prayer (P). Range pots boost your ranged for a while (R). Each bite or sip delays your next shot. On-screen buttons work too.

The title screen is now the West Bank. Tap tiles to walk, talk to a banker to use your bank, Veldan for the Shop, and Kael the Warden to start a fight. Wear gear from your inventory. Nothing real is ever charged.

Your account is shown as a chip on the title screen — it reads your adventurer name, or Guest. Use Sign out there if you want to switch accounts. Guests can fight Magmor, the Molten Titan in starter gear; sign in to unlock the rest and keep your progress.

Gloamhold

A sealed crypt-fortress under a drowned moon. Clear its rooms and defeat Morvane the Unsleeping to open the Vault Chest. Starter kit makes a clean clear unlikely — Black d'hide from the Shop, plus vault-forged kit and clean execution, can just clear it.

Rooms, in order: The Veil Gate → Supply Alcove → The Wailing Court → Supply Alcove → The Eclipse Corridor → The Last Alcove → The Hollow Throne → The Vault Chest. A small map at the top shows where you are. Dying lets you retry the room from when you entered it, or return to the hub. Loot still needs a Morvane kill.

Move: tap a floor tile to walk (one tile per tick, two if Run is on). A yellow X marks your destination. Keyboard A/D or arrow keys also work. Watch telegraphs and walk off marked tiles before they land.

Prayers — switch before the hit. Keys 1 / 2 / 3, or the buttons.

Magic (1): a violet rear-up, arms lifting, airy whoosh. Protect from Magic.

Missiles (2): a shard throw and a sharper mid cue. Protect from Missiles.

Melee (3): a sword slam and a low thud. Protect from Melee. Cinder Shades always melee.

The Veil Gate: rune tiles light in a sequence — step them in order (three rounds). Gloom lances mark your tile and land two ticks later; walk off. Then step the exit tile on the right.

The Wailing Court: the Pale Warden swaps Magic / Missiles with two-tick tells. Shades must be killed; if they live too long they heal him or explode on you.

The Eclipse Corridor: waves of marked tiles. Dodge, then step the exit.

Morvane — four phases (by remaining HP):

Twin Crowns (above 55%): Magic / Missiles, slower three-tick tells.

Court of Shades (28–55%): melee joins, faster tells. Shades spawn. Stand on the crown sigil or take Judgment. Don't stand still.

Nightfall (10–28%): quicker flicks, sometimes a one-tick tell, and an occasional Magic → Missiles → Melee combo. After a hit, your tile is marked — walk off. Seek the moon tile or your prayer drains. Rows of tiles go unsafe.

The Eye Opens (last 10%): enrage. Very fast, one-tick tells. Eye beams mark your tile. Prayer drains faster. Stillness is punished sooner.

Supplies: chests along the way are limited. F / Shark heals 20. P / Prayer pot restores 31 prayer. B / Brew heals 16 but costs 5 prayer. R / Range pot boosts your ranged. On-screen buttons work too. Each bite or sip delays your next shot.

Loot: living completers have a 1/8 chance of one Gloamhold unique from the vault (any of the 18 Eclipse, Unsleeping, or Hollowplate pieces — duplicates can drop and can be traded). The chest also pays score-based GP (2–8M) and a small supply cache. Later alcoves can grant mid-run supplies plus 100–200k GP. Older Hollowbolt and Veilhide pieces no longer drop but still work if you already have them. Spend GP in the Shop on Black d'hide. New gear goes to your bank — open Equipment on the hub to put it on.

Vaelkor the Stormwing

High in the Stormwing Eyrie, the stormwing general Vaelkor the Stormwing guards the Sky Citadel. Entry is open to any adventurer. Tier 2 gear is recommended — starter kit can get a kill, but it is slow and a missed prayer is deadly.

He flies. Only ranged or magic can hit him. Your crossbow is fine. Tap Vaelkor the Stormwing or a bodyguard to set your target (a red X). Tap a floor tile to move (yellow X). Keyboard A/D or arrows also work.

Prayers — switch before the hit. Keys 1 / 2 / 3, or the buttons. Listen for the tell.

Missiles (2): feathers and a tearing wind. This is his main attack and hits very hard (up to 71). Protect from Missiles blocks it.

Magic (1): a blue wind blast that punches through Protect from Missiles. You must flick to Protect from Magic or you will take it.

Melee (3): if you stand close (the middle tiles) he may dive, hit you, and knock you back one tile. Protect from Melee.

Tornadoes: he marks your tile with a swirling gale. Walk off before it lands — prayer will not save you from this one.

Bodyguards: Rask (Magic), Veyra (Missiles), and Korrin (Melee). They have little HP. Killing them first makes the fight cleaner; you can ignore them and go straight for Vaelkor the Stormwing if you prefer.

Supplies: bring food and potions from the bank provisioner — there are no free supplies here. Use the quick slots (pencil to edit). Each bite or sip delays your next shot. F / P / R, or the on-screen buttons.

Loot: every kill pays 150k–400k pretend GP. Stormwing helm, chestplate, chainskirt, and the Stormwing crossbow are a 1/6 unique roll per kill. If you already own a piece, the drop rerolls to one you do not. If you have the full set, the unique roll pays a 2–4M GP bonus instead of a duplicate piece. This is the only way to get that kit — it is never sold. Uniques go to your bank. Wear them from Equipment.

Widow Queen

Deep in a web-choked cave, the Widow Queen (Arachne) hunts anything that wanders in. She is a solo fight — about level 520, with 600 Hitpoints and a 3×3 footprint. Bring food and potions from the bank provisioner; there are no free supplies here.

Move and attack. The cave is a 5×5 grid. Tap the Queen, a baby spider, or a skeleton to set your target (a red X). Tap a floor tile to walk (yellow X). Keyboard A/D/W/S or arrows also work. Magic and ranged both hit her. The Widow Weaver wand and spellbook are built for this fight.

Prayers — switch before the hit. Keys 1 / 2 / 3, or the buttons. Listen for the tell.

Melee: when you stand next to her, her front legs lunge (up to 30). Protect from Melee blocks it.

Magic: from range she spits a venom bolt (up to 28). Protect from Magic blocks the hit. About 1 in 4 successful hits poisons you (starts at 6, then ticks down the usual way). Prayer does not block the poison itself — sip an Antipoison or wait it out. Antipoison also grants 90 seconds of immunity.

Web shot: about every 20 ticks she fires a web into the ceiling. A shadow marks the landing tile 3 ticks ahead — walk off it. If you are still there when it lands, you are stuck for 4 ticks (you can still eat and pray, but you cannot move or attack). Then 4–6 baby spiders drop and each bite hits 0–8 through prayer.

Below 50%: webs come a little faster (every 16 ticks). Every two or three specials she raises the dead: 2 skeletons of eaten victims crawl in from the far side, walk slowly, and shatter in one hit — a 5-tick weapon has time to shoot both before they arrive, but if one reaches you it hits up to 25 through prayer.

Below 20%: she enrages and drops two webs at once.

Loot: every kill pays 150k–400k pretend GP. The five Widow Weaver uniques (hood, robe top, robe bottom, wand, and spellbook) are a 1/6 roll per kill. If you already own a piece, the drop rerolls to one you do not. If you have the full set, the unique roll pays a 2–4M GP bonus instead of a duplicate piece. Uniques go to your bank. Wear them from Equipment. This kit is never sold.

The Hollow Knight-King

In a throne room flooding with shadow, the Hollow Knight-King still sits in his rusted crown. He is a solo fight — about level 520, with 600 Hitpoints. Bring food and potions from the bank provisioner; there are no free supplies here.

Move and attack. The hall is a 5×5 grid. Tap the king or a spectral knight to set your target. Tap a floor tile to walk. Keyboard A/D/W/S or arrows also work. Melee and ranged both hit him.

Prayers — switch before the hit. Keys 1 / 2 / 3, or the buttons.

Greatsword sweep: glowing tiles mark a 3-tile cone, then he slashes (up to 30). Walk off the marks. Protect from Melee blocks the hit if you are still on a marked tile.

Raise the guard: he plants his sword and a ring of spectral knights closes in. They are frail — kill them before they reach you, or Protect from Melee to block their hits (up to 12).

Shattered armour: around half health (or after enough specials) he breaks his own plate. He attacks faster, but takes 50% more damage.

Loot: every kill pays 150k–400k pretend GP. The five Kingsguard uniques (crowned full helm, platebody, platelegs, one-handed sword, and kiteshield) are a 1/6 roll per kill. If you already own a piece, the drop rerolls to one you do not. If you have the full set, the unique roll pays a 2–4M GP bonus instead of a duplicate piece. Uniques go to your bank. This kit is never sold.

Gorehorn, the Minotaur

In a stone labyrinth, Gorehorn charges anything that enters his hall. He is a solo fight — about level 520, with 600 Hitpoints. Bring food and potions from the bank provisioner; there are no free supplies here.

Move and attack. The maze is a 5×5 grid. Tap Gorehorn to set your target. Tap a floor tile to walk. Keyboard A/D/W/S or arrows also work. Melee and ranged both hit him.

Prayers — switch before the hit. Keys 1 / 2 / 3, or the buttons. Protect from Melee blocks his horn and his charge if you are still on the line.

Charge: a whole column lights up, then he thunders down it (up to 30). Step sideways off the marks to dodge. If the charge misses you, he slams into the far wall, is stunned for several ticks, and takes 50% more damage.

Stomp: he slams the floor and 4 tiles are marked. Rocks fall after 3 ticks (up to 25). Walk off the marks. Protect from Melee also blocks a rock that lands on you.

Loot: every kill pays 150k–400k pretend GP. The four Gorehorn uniques (horned helm, platebody, platelegs, and a massive two-handed war axe) are a 1/6 roll per kill. If you already own a piece, the drop rerolls to one you do not. If you have the full set, the unique roll pays a 2–4M GP bonus instead of a duplicate piece. The axe is slow (7 ticks) with a huge max hit. Wearing the full set makes your melee damage rise as your Hitpoints fall. Uniques go to your bank. This kit is never sold.

The Drowned Colossus

In a flooded pit, the Drowned Colossus lashes the water into killing waves. It is a solo fight — about level 520, with 600 Hitpoints. Bring food and potions from the bank provisioner; there are no free supplies here.

Move and attack. The pit is a 5×5 grid with raised rocks you can stand on. Tap the Colossus to set your target. Tap a floor tile to walk. Keyboard A/D/W/S or arrows also work. Melee and ranged both hit it.

Prayers — switch before the hit. Keys 1 / 2 / 3, or the buttons. Protect from Melee blocks its lashes, a wave if you miss the rock, a tentacle grab, and the eels.

Waves: a whole row lights up with foam, then the water sweeps it (up to 30). Stand on a rock in that row (or leave the row) to stay dry.

Tentacle grab: a tentacle pins you in place and hits you. Tap the screen to break free, or it slips after a few ticks.

Eels: two eels slither out of the depths and bite (up to 12). You can target and kill them.

Loot: every kill pays 150k–400k pretend GP. The five Colossus uniques (kraken helm, coral platebody, coral platelegs, a 4-tick tentacle whip, and a trident spear used as melee stab — not magic) are a 1/6 roll per kill. If you already own a piece, the drop rerolls to one you do not. If you have the full set, the unique roll pays a 2–4M GP bonus instead of a duplicate piece. The armor is attacker-focused: high accuracy, lighter defence. Uniques go to your bank. This kit is never sold.
