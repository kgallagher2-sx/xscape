Privacy Policy

Xscape · Updated November 10, 2026

Xscape is a free, non-commercial fan game shared with friends. It is not affiliated with Jagex.

Sign-in

You can sign in with Google, Discord, or GitHub, or continue as a guest. Sign-in is handled by Supabase.

What we store

We store:

  • Your account id
  • The email and/or display name provided by the sign-in service
  • The username you choose
  • Your game progress, including pretend GP, gear, kill counts, and leaderboard data

GP in Xscape is pretend and has no real-world value.

How we use it

This information is used only to run the game: saving your progress, showing the leaderboard, and letting you play with friends. We do not sell or share it. There are no ads and no tracking pixels.

Where it is held

Game data is held in Supabase, in the United States.

Deleting your data

To request deletion of your account and saved progress, open an issue at github.com/kgallagher2-sx/xscape/issues.
